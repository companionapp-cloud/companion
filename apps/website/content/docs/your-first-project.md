Notes and tasks pile up fast. Projects are how you gather the ones that belong together — and areas are how you group the projects.

## Areas and projects

In the sidebar, click **＋** beside **Areas** to create an *area*: a broad, long-running part of your life. "Work". "Home". Then click **＋** beside the area to add a *project* inside it — something with an end, like "v1.2 launch".

![The sidebar with an area and a project, and the project's own view](/docs/project-view.png)

Click an area or a project and it opens as a page of its own. (The little arrow beside an area folds its projects away; the name opens it.)

## The overview

Every area and project opens on an **overview**. Make it yours:

- **Add icon** gives it an emoji, which also shows up in the sidebar.
- **Add cover** puts an image across the top of the page.
- The description under the name is a full note editor. Format it, add a table, embed a file, and type `[[` to link a note, task or canvas.

Under that, the overview gathers what matters, ten rows at a time, with **View all** at the bottom of each card:

- A **project** shows its recently updated tasks, its upcoming tasks, and its recently updated notes and canvases — plus a progress ring that fills as its tasks get checked off.
- An **area** shows its projects, its **unsorted tasks** (the ones that live in the area itself rather than in one of its projects), and the upcoming tasks and notes from the whole area, its projects included.

The toolbar beside **Overview** opens the full lists. A project has Notes, Tasks, Lists, Canvases and Calendars; an area has Notes, Tasks and Canvases.

## Filing things

Anything can be filed after the fact — there's no need to decide up front. With a note, task or canvas open, click the **folder** icon in its toolbar and pick where it lives: an area, or a project. You can also drag a note or task from the list straight onto an area or a project in the sidebar, and select several at once to move them in one go.

Links drag the same way: a chip in a note or a task, a link or a preview in a chat, or a note or task card on a canvas (by the grip in its corner). Drop it on an area or a project to file what it points to. Drop a link to a project on an area to move the project into that area.

Everything lives in one place. Filing something in a project takes it out of wherever it was, so "which project is this in?" always has one answer. Not everything belongs to a project, which is what areas are for: a loose "book the dentist" can simply live in "Health".

Nothing has to be filed at all. The **Unsorted** filter at the top of Notes and Tasks is precisely the pile of things you haven't sorted — a useful place to work from, not a mess to feel bad about.

> **Tip:** Deleting a project or an area doesn't delete its contents by default — the notes and tasks inside simply become unsorted again. An area can be deleted once it has no projects left.

## What about habits?

Habits are in the app's plumbing but not yet in your hands: the **Habits** tab is a placeholder while streaks and gentle nudges get built. Until then, a repeating [task](/docs/your-first-task) is the closest thing.
