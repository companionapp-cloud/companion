Some thinking doesn't fit in a list. A canvas is an endless board where your notes, tasks and calendar events sit side by side with sticky notes, images and links — arranged however makes sense to you, with arrows between the things that relate.

## Create a canvas

Open **Canvases** in the sidebar and click the **＋** in the top right. A blank board opens; click its name at the top to call it something better than "Untitled canvas".

![A canvas with a group of notes and tasks, a sticky note, and arrows between them](/docs/canvas.png)

Drag the background to move around, and scroll or pinch to zoom. **Fit board** in the strip at the top (or **⌘0**) brings everything back into view when you've wandered off. On Windows and Linux, read every **⌘** on this page as **Ctrl**.

## Start with a sticky

Double-click anywhere on the board and a sticky note appears under your pointer, ready to type into. That's the fastest way to get a thought down: double-click, type, click away. Double-click a sticky again to edit it.

Drag a card to move it, and drag its corners to resize it. Cards snap to a fine grid so things line up without fuss — press **H** if you'd rather place them freely.

## Bring in your notes and tasks

The strip at the top of the board adds everything else. Each button has a one-key shortcut:

| Add | Key | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Sticky note** | **T** | A quick note that lives only on this board |
| **Group** | **G** | A labelled frame to gather cards in |
| **Note** | **N** | One of your notes, showing its title and first lines |
| **Task** | **K** | One of your tasks, with a checkbox that really ticks it off |
| **Event** | **E** | An event from a [connected calendar](/docs/calendars) |
| **Image** | **I** | A picture from your files |
| **Link** | **L** | A web page, with its title, description and image |

Note, task and event cards aren't copies. They show the real thing: tick a task on the board and it's done everywhere, and double-clicking a card opens the note or task itself. Deleting a card only takes it off the board — the note or task behind it is untouched.

Two shortcuts save a trip to the strip. **Paste** a web address to get a link card, or an image to get an image card. And you can **drag** a note or a task out of any list and drop it straight onto the board. To take one the other way, hover its card and drag the grip in the top-right corner onto an area or a project in the sidebar, or a task onto the agenda on [Today](/docs/today-home-base).

## Connect things

Hover over a card and a small handle appears on each side. Drag from a handle to another card to draw an arrow between them. Or select two cards and press **C**.

Double-click an arrow to label it. With an arrow selected, the bar at the bottom of the board changes its line — curved, stepped or straight — and what sits at each end: an arrow, a dot, or nothing. To point an arrow somewhere else, drag its end onto a different card.

## Group and colour

Select a few cards — **Shift**-drag draws a box around them, **⌘**-click adds one at a time — and press **G** to wrap them in a group. Double-click the group's label to name it. A group carries whatever is inside it, so dragging the group moves the whole cluster, and anything you drop into it later comes along too.

With something selected, the swatches colour it, and the number keys **1**–**9** do the same from the keyboard (**0** goes back to the default). Colour works on stickies, groups and arrows alike, which makes it a cheap way to say "these belong together" or "this one's at risk".

> **Tip:** Press **?** on a board for the full list of shortcuts — there are keys for aligning and spacing cards, duplicating, bringing things to the front, and finding a card by name (**⌘K**). **⌘Z** undoes anything.

## File it, find it

A canvas is filed like everything else: click the **folder** icon at the top of the board to put it in an area or a [project](/docs/your-first-project), where it shows up under the project's **Canvases**. Boards also appear in [the graph](/docs/using-the-graph), linked to every note and task you've placed on them.

Deleting a canvas moves it to the Trash for 30 days. The notes and tasks on it stay exactly where they were.

On your phone, the board works the same way with your fingers: the add buttons move to a bar at the bottom, with **More** holding notes, tasks, events, images and links.
