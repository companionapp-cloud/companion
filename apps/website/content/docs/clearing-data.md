Both live in **Settings → Danger Zone**. Everything there is permanent, so each clear asks you to type **delete** first.

## Clear data

Each row clears one tool: notes, tasks, canvases, chats, calendars, or areas and projects. **Clear all data** clears everything, including object types, AI agents, calendar accounts and exports.

- Cleared items skip the Trash. They can't be restored.
- Signed in, a clear reaches all your devices and the server with the next sync. Signed out, it only affects this device.
- Clearing notes also deletes the files only those notes use. A file a task, a canvas or a cover still uses stays.
- Clearing calendars removes your calendar accounts and subscriptions from Companion. Nothing is deleted from iCloud, Google or any other calendar provider.
- Clearing areas and projects keeps what was filed in them. Those notes and tasks become unfiled.

Want a copy first? [Export your workspace](/docs/exporting) before you clear it.

## Delete your account

When you're signed in, **Delete account** asks for your password, then signs out every device, this one included. Your account and everything in it are deleted 30 days later. You'll get an email with the date.

Changed your mind? Sign in on any device before then and your account is restored as it was.

On Companion Cloud, your subscription stops renewing as soon as you ask, and is cancelled when the account is deleted. Sign in again while it's still active and renewal turns back on.

Data already on your devices stays there after they're signed out. To remove it too, use **Clear all data** on each device.
